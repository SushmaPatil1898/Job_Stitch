Job Title: Clinical Data Programmer II

We are looking for a Clinical Data Programmer to join our Biometrics team.
The ideal candidate has hands-on experience with SDTM and ADaM datasets,
strong SQL and Python skills, and familiarity with CDISC standards and
regulatory submissions (FDA, EMA, PMDA).

Responsibilities:
- Develop and validate SDTM and ADaM datasets for clinical trials
- Write Define.xml specifications and support regulatory submissions
- Perform data validation and quality checks using SQL and Python
- Collaborate with Biostatistics, Data Management, and Clinical Operations
- Support automation initiatives to improve programming efficiency

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in clinical data programming
- Proficiency in SQL, SAS, and/or Python
- Strong understanding of CDISC, SDTM, and ADaM standards
- Experience with regulatory submissions (FDA, EMA, PMDA) preferred
- Excellent communication and cross-functional collaboration skills
- Experience with AWS or cloud data pipelines is a plus
